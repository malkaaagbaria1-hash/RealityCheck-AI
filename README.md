# RealityCheck AI

AI-powered scam detection for links, messages and files — in Arabic, Hebrew and English.

**One page:** paste a link or message, or choose a file, and get a clear answer:
🚨 Likely a scam · ⚠️ Suspicious · ✅ No scam signs — with the reasons and what to do.

## What it checks
- **Links:** the real site behind the link, fake brand names (e.g. `paypa1`), payment/login pages on unknown sites, risky domain endings, shortened links.
- **Messages:** pressure, threats, requests for passwords or money, fake organizations (police, post, banks).
- **Files:** disguised programs (`invoice.pdf.exe`), Office macros, PDFs that run code, phishing pages, programs hidden in archives, Android apps from outside the store.
